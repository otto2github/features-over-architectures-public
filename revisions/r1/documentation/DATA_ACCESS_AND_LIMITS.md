# Data access and reproducibility limits

The manuscript and Technical Appendix identify CSMAR STK_Violation_Main, FIN_Audit, PLED_TRDDETL, HLD_Contrshr and RPT_Operation; Tushare financial/holder/manager endpoints; and CNINFO annual-report metadata. Access requires the relevant provider/account or institutional authorization. Research authorization is not a redistribution licence for raw or scored company rows.

The predecessor construction sources, fields, aggregate outputs and historical loader evidence are extracted under semantic paths in this archive. See `documentation/predecessor/data_access.md`, `documentation/predecessor/raw_input_fields.csv`, `code/predecessor/` and `code/historical_reference/`. No nested predecessor ZIP or historical download is needed for the public checks. A current provider download need not recover original source vintages.

The distribution includes per-fit aggregate results and retained aggregate resampling arrays, not row-level labels/predictions/checkpoints/private masks. It supports aggregate regeneration and limited synthetic interface checks, not empirical reconstruction without separate authorized inputs.

Original sensitivity Stage E did not explicitly enforce strict algorithms in each process. Its isolated replay matched 45 MLP arrays exactly; ten GNN arrays differed, with the stated reporting and ranking qualifications retained. Original scores remain the reporting basis. The component rule was specified beforehand but its membership table was materialized after fitting; packaging does not backdate it.

No new public deposit has been made by this build. Historical identifiers are distinguished in `provenance/release.json`. Restricted source entry points require private inputs and their execution environment; they are not portable install recipes or permission to overwrite any environment. Public checks do not run those entry points.


The E7 addition distributes conservative record-incidence aggregate summaries and marked source copies only. Hashed counterparty record keys are pseudonymous private data and are excluded together with edge rows, support-node mappings, labels and scores. Source identifiers are not independently validated global legal-entity keys. Its 2,000 fixed-score paired bootstrap draws were executed privately; only summaries, not the draw arrays, are public.
